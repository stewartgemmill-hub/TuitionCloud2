Quester

Quester is a brilliant application for helping you learn just about any new topic or skill set. Simply give the topic a name, then create a question and answer that you wish to be used to test your understanding. You can also enhance the experience by including a hint and additional information to access during or after each test. The tests will be relayed back to the User in a radom fashion with or without timers and passwords. Users can also share their tests with specific Users like for example a study group, or share them with the online community in general. Users can even monitor the performance of invited test participants and compile similar tests that focus only on incorrect answers. For a full list of features and how they operate including free access to the online forum, check out the DEMO section from the Main menu. Enjoy!
